## 🚀 Overview
Karaoke Software for Windows is a friendly sing-along player that turns your PC into a karaoke machine. It plays CDG and MP3+G tracks with lyrics that scroll in time, manages a queue of singers, and lets you nudge the key up or down to match any voice. Built for home parties, practice sessions, and small venues alike, this karaoke software keeps the night flowing with playlists and quick search. Load your track library and everyone gets a turn at the mic.

## ✨ Features
- **Scrolling lyrics** — Show synced, easy-to-read lyrics on screen so singers never lose their place.
- **Singer queue** — Line up who is next and keep the rotation moving smoothly all night.
- **Key and tempo control** — Shift the pitch or slow the tempo to fit each singer's range.
- **Dual-screen output** — Send lyrics to a second screen or TV while you control the show on your PC.
- **Playlists and search** — Organize your track library and find any song in seconds.
